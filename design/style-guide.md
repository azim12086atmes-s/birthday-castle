## 1. Style in one sentence

**Realistic shapes, painted surfaces, magical light.** Real-world scale and proportions like Zelda BotW/TotK, but every surface is a soft, simplified color field (Ghibli), lit at night by glowing accents instead of detailed textures (Keeper's bioluminescence). (A1, A2, A5)

### Style rules
1. **Silhouette first.** Every building, tree and island must read from its outline alone. The castle is the strongest silhouette in the world. (C1)
2. **Simplify detail, never scale.** Big forms are realistic; small detail is removed and replaced with painted color shifts. Low contrast in small details, high contrast only at focal points. (C4, A4)
3. **Soft rounded nature.** Trees are puffy ball-shaped canopies; clouds are huge soft cumulus; hills roll gently. Avoid sharp, noisy, spiky shapes except gothic spires. (F1, S1, T1)
4. **Rich ornament on architecture and clothing only.** Gothic tracery, Islamic geometric patterns, Gaudí-style tile mosaics, embroidered dresses (Genshin). Ornament is where the eye should linger. (B1, B2, B4, A3)
5. **Each area has its own palette** (Split Fiction, kidswithsticks emotion palettes). (A9, C5)
6. **Characters**: cel-shaded, two-tone light and shadow with a rim light; no eyes on creatures per the brief. (A3, A4)

## 2. Lighting

| Layer | What it does | How (cheap on laptops) |
|---|---|---|
| **Moonlight key** | Soft cool-blue directional light from high and to one side, so the castle has a lit face and a shadow face | 1 directional light, the only real-time shadow caster, shadows only near the player |
| **Sky fill** | Indigo-violet ambient so shadows are colored, never black | Hemisphere light: violet sky, deep teal ground |
| **Warm accents** | Gold windows, lanterns, candles: pockets of safety and warmth | Emissive materials, no real lights (B6, O1) |
| **Bioluminescence** | Cyan and magenta glow on tree trunk patterns, flowers, crystals, pathway gems | Emissive masks that gently pulse (T7, F6) |
| **Foliage never black** | Small self-glow on all plants | Slight emissive boost (F8) |
| **Rim light** | Bright edge on characters and hero objects against the sky | Fresnel term in the toon shader (C7) |
| **Atmosphere** | Distance turns pale indigo, fog cards separate depth layers | Height fog with a colored tint + a few transparent fog planes (S4) |
| **Grade** | Final film look | One LUT pass (S5) |

**Palette (night):** base indigo `#1b1840` → violet `#3a2d6b` → teal `#1f5560`; accents gold `#ffc65c`, bioluminescent cyan `#5ef2ff`, soft magenta `#ff7ad9`; stained glass in ruby, sapphire, emerald, amber. This is the night version of Remains' orange/blue complementary scheme. (C6)

**Before midnight Oct 23 (dusk):** warmer sky gradient (peach → lilac), aurora faint. **After midnight:** full aurora, galaxy, stardust strokes, everything glows brighter.

**Avoid (too heavy for the web):** many real lights, real-time GI, ray tracing, reflections, shadows on grass and bushes. (P5, P8)

## 3. Layout

Composition from Magic Moorland, the Blender cinematic article and BotW's opening view. (C2, C3, B3, B9)

```
            ☁  ☁  floating islands ring the castle, each with its own palette  ☁
                    ↑ portals / glowing bridges of light
   painted far mountains ─────────────── sea of clouds below the islands
                  [ CASTLE on a raised plateau, symmetric, the focal point ]
                          formal courtyard: fountain, parterres
                                 ↑ grand stairs
                      [ MAGIC ARCH GATE ]  ← first big reveal
                                 ↑
           glowing gem pathway winds through bioluminescent forest
                                 ↑
                       spawn meadow (wide view of everything)
```

1. **Spawn with a reveal.** Players arrive in an open meadow where the castle, islands and aurora are all visible at once, like BotW's first view of the Great Plateau.
2. **Everything points at the castle.** The pathway, river, stardust particle streams and cloud lines all lead toward it. (O4, C3)
3. **Three depth layers everywhere.** Darker framed foreground (tree shadows), saturated midground, pale fogged background. (C2)
4. **The gate is a threshold.** Pointed gothic arch with a patterned crown and levitating stones; passing it changes the mood from forest to palace. (B1, B2, B8)
5. **Courtyard as a French-style formal garden** with a central fountain; symmetric, so the castle reads as monumental. (B9, B3)
6. **Floating islands as destinations**, reached by portals or light bridges, each a different biome and palette (crystal island, flower island, celebration island). (T3, O2, A9)
7. **Distance is painted.** Far mountains, forests and clouds are cards, so the world feels vast while staying light. (T2, F7, S1)
