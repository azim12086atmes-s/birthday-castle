# Our rock and cliff models, sorted (Oct 17)

Sheet: [rock-assets-sheet.jpg](rock-assets-sheet.jpg). The numbers below match the tiles. All of these are Meshy models in `main/meshy/`.

| Class | Models (sheet no.) | What they're good for on the plateaus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sheer cliff faces** | Rocky_Cliffside (3), Cliff_Formations (4), Mesa_Cliff_Wall (1), Stone (23), Elephant_Rock_Formati (21) | Facing pieces for the step walls: fluted columns, sharp slabs, overhangs. Cliff_Formations (tall mossy flutes) and Rocky_Cliffside (layered sandstone with a carved corner) are the best match. |
| **Buttes and pinnacles** | Majestic_Rock_Formati (20), rocky_terrain_with_te (6), Mountain (13), Devils_Tower, Mesa_View (2) | Crowns for the plateau tops and corners. Majestic is already terraced, like a small version of what the owner wants. |

| **Boulders and scree** | Rocky_Terrain (5), Desert_Rock_Formation (18), Desert_Monolith (17), Rock_Formation_Collec, Rock_Formations_Colle | Rubble at the foot of each step. |
| **Crystals** | Prismatic_Peaks (25), Crystal_Peaks_PBR (26) | Glowing veins and clusters on the cliff faces at night. |
| **Caves and arches** | Cave_Entrance (27), Cave_Structures_1 (28), Cave_Formations_Colle (29), Rocky_Archway (30), Desert_Sandstone_Arch (31), A_large_arch_made_fr (32) | Openings cut into the lower steps. |
| **Terrain tiles** | Terrain_mesh (7), Make_a_terrain (8), Fantasy_Terrain_Bases (9), Riverbank_Terrain_Mod (10), Erosion_Pathway (11), Mountain_Range (14), a_large_mountain_val (15) | Too small or too flat for the cliffs; better as close-up ground dressing. |
| **Floating** | Floating_Stone_Island (33), Pine_Trees_on_a_Rocky (34) | Sky isles, not the cliffs. |

Plan for the plateaus: make the step walls curved and sharp (fluted columns, overhanging lips, a jagged crest, faceted shading). Give the plateau outlines spurs and inlets instead of round blobs. Then crown edges and ledges with pieces cut from the cliff-face and butte classes.
