# The story in scenes

Every scene is a live camera in the game world, played for all friends at the same moment, with letterbox bars and soft babble voices with subtitles. {her} stands for her name. A full place visit has about 90 seconds of scenes in roughly 10 minutes of play.

## The big scenes

### Arrival ride and aerial reveal (60 s, 20 s on repeats)

*When:* a player joins.

1. **Wide**, 6 s. Dawn at the sea docks outside the crescent; her ride waits, sparkling.
2. **Tracking**, 8 s. The ride sets off across the water towards the white waterfall wall.
3. **Close up**, 5 s. The waterfall veil parts like curtains; light pours through.
4. **Pov**, 6 s. Riding through the falls; spray, sparkles, then sudden calm.
5. **Establishing aerial**, 14 s. The camera soars up: the crescent of mountains, 32 places, the palace glowing in the middle.
6. **Flyover**, 9 s. Down along the long marble bridge; each step ripples in neon colours.
7. **Crane up**, 7 s. Up the palace facade, gold petal arch, purple domes.
8. **Dolly in**, 5 s. The giant doors swing open.

### Her welcome (25 s)

*When:* the honoree reaches the palace doors.

1. **Wide**, 6 s. The great hall: her friends waiting in their party outfits.
2. **Close up**, 4 s. Her friends turn and cheer.
3. **Crane up**, 7 s. Confetti, petals and lanterns fill the hall; the banner reads Happy Birthday {her}.
4. **Two shot**, 8 s. The Head Courtier bows. *“Welcome, {her}. Tonight, this whole world is yours.”*

### The Mew bonds with her (25 s)

*When:* after her welcome.

1. **Insert**, 4 s. A small pink glow tumbles down from the chandelier.
2. **Close up**, 6 s. The Mew, eyeless and smiling, floats upside down in front of her, tail wagging.
3. **Two shot**, 7 s. It boops her nose and mews. *“Mew!”*
4. **Orbit**, 8 s. It circles her once, and a ribbon of light ties them together.

### The flying ship docks with the men (20 s)

*When:* the men join.

1. **Wide**, 5 s. A shadow passes over the palace terrace.
2. **Crane up**, 8 s. The flying ship sails out of the clouds, sails glowing.
3. **Tracking**, 7 s. It docks at the terrace; the men step down the gangway.

### The Hush falls (45 s)

*When:* 21:10 or the host starts it.

1. **Wide**, 6 s. Everyone gathers under the Great Birthday Lantern.
2. **Crane up**, 6 s. The sky darkens; a cold grey fog rolls over the mountains.
3. **Close up**, 6 s. A crack runs across the lantern.
4. **Wide**, 8 s. The lantern shatters into 32 star shards that streak out across the world.
5. **Establishing aerial**, 8 s. Colour drains from the land, place by place.
6. **Close up**, 6 s. The Hush, a jealous shadow of fog and grey glass, speaks. *“Bring the light home before midnight, or the birthday will never come.”*
7. **Two shot**, 5 s. The Mew tugs her sleeve and points to the first place.

### The Birthday Tournament (20 s)

*When:* arrival at B08.

1. **Establishing aerial**, 6 s. The colosseum, banners in every colour.
2. **Tracking**, 8 s. The friends walk out into the arena; griffins fly overhead.
3. **Title card**, 6 s. The herald announces the champions.

### Return to the palace (30 s)

*When:* the last shard before the final boss.

1. **Establishing aerial**, 10 s. Night falls; every freed people travels to the palace in long lines of light.
2. **Flyover**, 10 s. Gondolas, longboats, dragons, sky whales and lanterns converge on the palace.
3. **Wide**, 10 s. The Hush waits in the heart of the palace.

### Midnight finale and the reveal (300 s)

*When:* celebrationAt.

1. **Wide**, 20 s. The clock strikes midnight; the choir begins.
2. **Crane up**, 30 s. The glass roof opens like a flower.
3. **Tracking**, 40 s. The floor rises into the sky; pavilions and waterfalls form a floating theatre.
4. **Flyover**, 40 s. Jellyfish swarms, sky whales and dragons breathing flame light the sky.
5. **Close up**, 30 s. The Mew cuts the giant cake with ribbons of light.
6. **Wide**, 30 s. Slices float to every guest by magic.
7. **Two shot**, 40 s. The Mew glows gold and grows into the owner's human avatar.
8. **Close up**, 30 s. He calls her name. *“Happy birthday, {her}.”*
9. **Crane up**, 40 s. Everyone she saved fills the theatre and cheers.

## Every place visit

Each place plays these scenes in order. A place with a boss adds the boss entrance before the rescue.

### Travelling to the next place (20 s, 6 s on repeats)

*When:* the group sets off from a freed place.

1. **Tracking**, 6 s. The group rides out together; the ship or their rides lift off.
2. **Flyover**, 8 s. Aerial journey over the crescent; the Mew flies ahead and looks back.
3. **Title card**, 6 s. The next place rises over the horizon; its name writes itself in gold light.

### Arriving at a place (15 s, 5 s on repeats)

*When:* the group arrives at a place.

1. **Establishing aerial**, 6 s. Slow orbit high over the place, drained to grey by the Hush.
2. **Crane down**, 5 s. Camera sinks to the plaza; the creatures hide.
3. **Wide**, 4 s. The group lands; the local runs up to them.

### Meeting the people (15 s, 8 s on repeats)

*When:* after arriving.

1. **Two shot**, 8 s. The local greets her and the group.
2. **Over shoulder**, 7 s. The local points to the frozen head.

### The frozen head (12 s, 6 s on repeats)

*When:* the group reaches the head.

1. **Dolly in**, 7 s. Slow push in to the head, frozen in grey glass; a faint glow pulses inside.
2. **Close up**, 5 s. The voice from inside the glass.

### Befriending the creatures (8 s, 3 s on repeats)

*When:* a creature is befriended for the first time here.

1. **Close up**, 4 s. The creature peeks out shyly, then comes closer.
2. **Orbit**, 4 s. It circles her and settles beside the group; a capture is taken.

### Boss entrance (12 s)

*When:* a boss challenge starts.

1. **Wide**, 4 s. The ground shakes; the sky darkens over the place.
2. **Crane up**, 5 s. The boss rises to full height.
3. **Title card**, 3 s. The boss's name slams onto the screen.

### The head is freed (15 s, 8 s on repeats)

*When:* the challenge is won.

1. **Close up**, 3 s. Cracks of light run through the grey glass.
2. **Wide**, 4 s. The glass bursts into petals; colour washes outward over the whole place.
3. **Two shot**, 8 s. The head thanks her.

### The star shard (8 s, 4 s on repeats)

*When:* after the head is freed.

1. **Crane up**, 4 s. A star shard rises out of the place and floats to her hands.
2. **Insert**, 4 s. The shard flies up to the lantern in the sky; the count ticks up (5 of 32).

### Farewell (8 s, 4 s on repeats)

*When:* the group leaves.

1. **Wide**, 8 s. The head and the people set off towards the palace, waving.
