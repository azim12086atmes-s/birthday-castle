## 0. Scale: the owner chose the labelled heights (2026-10-11)

Measured on sheet 03, the drawing is 800 m wide at about 1.16 m per pixel. At that scale, the drawn heights come out about 2.7× smaller than the labelled heights. For example, the tallest spire is drawn at about 400 m but labelled 1100 m, and the cone pavilion is drawn at about 250 m but labelled 700 m.

| Option | Width | Tallest spire | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: as drawn | 800 m | about 400 m | Looks exactly like the sketch. |
| **B: labelled heights** *(chosen)* | 800 m | 1100 m | Every tower is about 2.7× taller and thinner than drawn. |

**The owner chose B.** Every height below is given as drawn, so **multiply all heights by `H_SCALE` = 2.75**. That gives spires of 840, 960 and 1100 m, a cone pavilion of 700 m, a great dome finial of about 960 m, and a front wall of about 425 m. Plan sizes and widths stay as listed, so the towers come out taller and thinner than drawn.

## 1. Footprint and levels (crops 08 and 09)

- **Plan:** 800 m east–west × 680 m north–south. The front faces south, down the golden causeway.
- **Levels:**

| Level | Height | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| L0, the podium | 0 to 45 m | Two storeys of round-arched arcades with rose planters on every sill. The cascade stair cuts through it. |
| L1, the main front | 45 to 155 m | The gate, four great lancets, wall buttresses with pinnacles. A rose-garland parapet on top. |
| L2, the roof garden level | about 155 m | The whole roof is gardens, stepping up to the north in 3 or 4 terraces. Each terrace edge has a rose parapet, cypresses and pink blossom trees. |
| L3, the towers | rising out of L2 | Domes, needle spires, and the cone pavilion. |

- **The plan (crop 08), in order of importance:**
  1. The great onion dome sits **west of centre**.
  2. Two long N–S water channels with fountains run from the gate to the north.
  3. **Two eight-spoke wheel parterres** sit in the east half.
  4. **Two glass conservatories** sit in the NE.
  5. Domed corner towers stand at all four corners, plus gold domes midway along the east and west sides.
  6. Everything else is square garden beds framed by 3 m paths.
- **Rear (crop 06):** 3 or 4 **waterfalls** spill off the north and east terrace edges into pools at the foot.

## 3. Roof gardens and the rear (crops 06, 07, 08, 09 and sheet 16)

- **Cone pavilion** on the north axis: about 250 m drawn (labelled 700 m). A tall **purple tent-cone roof** with gold ribs and a star finial on an arcaded pavilion. Spirelets ring its base. Waterfalls pour from its terrace.
- **Glass conservatories** ×2 (crop 07): long barrel vaults with a cross-gable, white or gold ribs and pale blue glass. Orange trees grow inside.
- **Courtyard fountains:** round basins with tiered A035 lotus fountains at the centre of each court.
- **The G02 roof court** (sheet 16; spec §2.1): West Rose Terrace, North Fountain Loggia, East Conservatory. The labelled sizes win.
- **Terrace planting:** on every terrace, a row of **cypresses** (T02a) about every 15 m, **pink blossom trees** (T01) in carved planters, and **rose garlands** draped over every parapet.

## 5. How to model it so a laptop copes

- **Kitbash from modules.** Model one of each and instance it:
  - Arch panel bay, lancet, buttress with pinnacle.
  - Drum turret in 3 sizes.
  - Onion dome in 3 sizes.
  - Needle spire stage.
  - Corner pavilion.
  - Balustrade metre, garland metre, planter.
  - The trees.
- **Carving and tracery** go into baked normal and alpha maps ("sculpt high, bake low", sheet 07). Window glass is one emissive indigo material with a tracery alpha.
- **Budget:**

| Distance | Triangles |
|---|---|
| Full detail, within about 300 m | about 120k |
| Mid (300 to 1500 m) | 20k |
| Far (over 1500 m) | 2k impostor |

- **Instance the counts:** 400+ spirelets, 100+ cypresses and blossom trees, 60+ lanterns.

- That spirelets crowd the skyline the way they do on the sheet.
