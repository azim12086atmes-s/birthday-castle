# Review of the Meshy models: what to use, what to tweak (Oct 16)

Judged from the kit sheets in this folder (renders of the exact models). Colours and details are from the pictures, so treat anything small as "check on close-up".

## Are we following the maps?
- **Yes for the 20 km plan (sheet 04):** coast and relief traced from it, B01 to B25 at the spec positions, golden causeway, sky isles I, II, IV, V.
- **No for the branch's 12 km maps (WLD-MAP-001/002):** kept as reference only. They hold things our map does not: three forest districts (F01 to F03), eight cottage clearings (FC01 to FC08), ponds FD01 to FD03, trails FT01 to FT03, the fjord and misty hills (T06), jade wetlands (T04).
- **Not yet from any map:** where the scattered pieces go (forest cottages, Ember trees, stone circles, English cottages) is a random scatter on land, not a map position. Recommended next: move the branch's forest districts and clearings onto our 20 km map (same relative spot) and place the cottages there.
- **Known gaps:** Sky Isle III is missing from our map; the coast is ±150 m; mountains are guessed from colour.

## Must change before use
- **Classical_Sculpture_C:** the busts have faces. Veil or remove them, and split the file into its six parts (two urns, two busts, two small figures).
- **Gothic_Iron_Fence:** cross finials become stars; copper to gold and white.
- **Any lion or koi:** eyes check is still open.

## Not usable (yet)
- Nothing at palace scale: no Meshy model replaces the palace itself. The palace stays our own build (B01 sheets).
- Small props (3 m fence, urns, bonsai) cannot be seen on a 20 km view; they only show in close-ups.

## Heavy models
Triangle counts are 0.1 to 4.7 million each. Fine for Unreal with Nanite; too heavy for a laptop. The renders here use reduced copies, the saved files keep the exact meshes.
