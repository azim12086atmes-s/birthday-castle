# The 162 models that hadn't been opened (Oct 16)

The owner's GitHub `main/meshy/` now holds 201 distinct models. 39 were already in use or opened (batch 1). The other 162 were downloaded one by one and rendered from two sides on a grey ground: `sheet-01.jpg` to `sheet-08.jpg`, numbered as in `index.json`. Each tile shows triangle count and shape proportions. Two could not be read: `Arc_en_Rêve_arch` (its accented folder name breaks the download) and `Magenta_Bonsai_Tree` (broken file).

Where each one goes is in `art-references/world-fill/fill_plan.json` and, per place, in `game-schema/game-data.json` (`places[].assets`, `assets[]` with notes and fixes).
