    How to play

    W A S D or arrows to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump.

    Drag to look around, scroll to zoom. On a phone, use your left thumb to move.

    Click the glowing path to make it ripple. T to twirl.

    Step into a portal ring to ride a lotus sky-boat to a floating island.

    Stardust Hunt: collect the golden star shards hidden on every island.

    Wish Lanterns: at Lantern Lake's shrine, press E to send a wish into the sky.

    Turn on the microphone to talk to players near you.
