## Rejected redraw errors

- Bare open arcades replaced solid glazed palace walls.
- Decoration was later restored, but wing massing, tier offsets and tower attachments still changed.
- A small dome placed at a wing end does not substitute for the broad western onion pavilion above/behind the original range.
- West and east ranges have unequal lengths, different projections and different upper structures.
- Matching glass and marble colours alone does not preserve the original architecture.

## Working rule

Preserve these original references. Establish source correspondence before any new structural synthesis. The existing component masters decorate the actual source layout; they do not determine a new layout. Roof/garden placement and hidden depths must be resolved explicitly, not silently invented. Other reserved structural generations are paused for this review.
