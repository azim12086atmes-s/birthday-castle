## Components visible in the overview that the generic kit does not resolve

- The longitudinal elevation has eight visible lower arch openings: four narrower outer openings and four broader middle openings. This visual count is not a measured span schedule.
- The central pair of wide arches meets at a very slender central support, distinct from the broad lotus cutwater piers elsewhere. The current segment sheet does not isolate this support.
- Both ends have shore approach transitions rather than a bridge terminating as an arbitrary flat deck slab. Their exact gradients and hidden joins are not specified by the source panels.
- Standing lantern posts, planter/flower placements and local parapet ornaments follow a placement rhythm. A decorative parts pack alone does not define that layout.
- The user-requested stepped lotus capital, now A03, comes from MOD-COL-001/GEN-29. Keep it as a requested supplemental design component; do not present it as a literal extraction of this bridge's original pier head.

## Ambiguities inside the source illustration

- The top-left view is labelled plan view but shows visible side arches. It is an illustrative view rather than a registered orthographic footprint.
- D's front/isometric emphasize the lotus lower body, while its side drawing depicts stacked open structural stages. These views need reconciliation when resolving the underlying geometry.
- The 14 m C module does not account for all the visibly broader spans in the longitudinal elevation. A single uniform arch pitch cannot be assumed.
- The overall height and stacked tier/rail labels need reconciliation. Preserve the printed labels without claiming they already form a consistent dimension schedule.
- The 4 m tile grid needs border/cut-strip decisions across the 13 m central deck band and 2.5 m side bands. This is a layout task, not permission to redraw the mosaic.

## Next correction scope

Hold new unrelated asset generation. Use these direct crops as the reference register. Correct the cutwater silhouette, freestanding lantern post and tile pattern; resolve the narrow/wide arch families, central support and end approaches before claiming complete bridge coverage. A corrected structural sheet can retain four principal families, while a 10–20-part detail sheet covers the actual source ornaments. Do not generate another isolated sheet for every tiny component.

The current kits remain saved design interpretations. They are not verified matches to GEN-18 and should not be treated as ready references for an exact bridge reconstruction.
