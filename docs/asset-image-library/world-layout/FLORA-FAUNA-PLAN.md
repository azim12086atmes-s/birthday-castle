# Location-specific flora and fauna, shared natural kits

Each place gets its own **plant and wildlife selection**. Water, rocks, soil, banks, paths, roots and other common natural forms come from a general library, with local materials and placement. Repeated species count once; every building does not automatically require another animal sheet.

## Production rules

Inspect one source at a time and preserve original pixels/prompts. Generate only an identified missing form or unresolved construction view. Use 10–20 readable small subjects or 3–4 larger forms with useful views; do not fill unused cells with invented items. Species, anatomy and pose should remain consistent across views; exact botanical/anatomical validity is not proved by concept art. Living coral is fauna, fungi are a separate natural-life category, and sculpted animal/coral ornaments are not wildlife models.
