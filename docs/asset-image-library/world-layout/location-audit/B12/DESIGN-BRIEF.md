## What the image shows

A cluster of cream-plaster/carved-timber houses with steep blue-violet slate gables, dormers, chimneys, arched doors/windows, rose boxes and an arched street passage between attached buildings. Miniature isometric suggests a compact joined settlement, not isolated identical cottages.

## Proposed layout and experience

A winding cobbled lane enters through the source arched passage, opens into a small shared flower court, and branches into two lanes toward windmill/forest. Keep taller gabled corner houses as markers and quieter small cottages along edges; rear gardens connect via footpaths without becoming giant plazas.

This paragraph describes an imagined, source-compatible arrangement. Hidden backs, room depths, elevations and exact routes are not recovered measurements.
