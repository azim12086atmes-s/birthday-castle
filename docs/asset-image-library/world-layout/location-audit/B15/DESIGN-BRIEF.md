## What the image shows

A steep jade/verdigris stepped temple with central stairs, gold seam bands, lotus-like roof crest and pointed gilded portals at successive terraces. Palms and fern planting frame the broad base; isometric shows compact setbacks rather than separate boxed wings.

## Proposed layout and experience

Use a small shaded clearing as arrival, one strong central stair for the hero approach and a quieter side route linking terrace landings. Plant pockets flank entrances rather than blocking portals; make the upper shrine a framed destination with depth. Hidden rear return is inferred.
