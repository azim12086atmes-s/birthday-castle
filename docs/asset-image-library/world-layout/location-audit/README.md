# Location audit — source zooms and proposed designs

**34 places reviewed:**25 named landmarks,6 special sites and 3 forest districts. Each brief separates visible source features, imagined layout, required structures, local flora/fauna, reusable natural objects and gaps. Source pictures remain unchanged. These are design briefs, not measured site plans or complete models.

Only one crop was inspected at a time. Architectural panels or front details were reviewed for all 25 landmarks; sky/forest/lake/meadow crops establish their concept or planning context. The index also saves 24 oldermapcontext crops for later comparison; not all of those extra crops were viewed individually. OriginalGEN-10 lake was additionally reviewed. ## Source discrepancies that matter

- GEN-37 remains the palace front authority; its cascade, stairs, connected bays and lateral towers are preserved. Rear roof/courtyard geometry is still proposed.
- GEN-05/06 detailed building sheets control appearance where GEN-04 tiny map drawings differ. Misty Keep is a timber/teal gallery complex; Pillar City uses unequal rock pillars/open buildings and rope bridges.
- Temple Hill’s front shows two prominent rotundas, while its isometric suggests a rear third. Lighthouse’s spiral band is not proven to be a walkable stair. Starfall/Aurora miniatures do not supply complete interiors.
- GEN-10’s 3.6×1.3 km lake scene does not match the current 650×700 m western lake reservation. Reuse its shore/cove/ghat/water components without silently replacing the current map.
- B12’s existing kits are one adapted cottage subtype, not a completed joined village. Crystal Conservatory’s floor/footrail must match the correct subtype before palette adoption.
- New forest symbols and sky projections are placement proposals. FD01 pond/stream form, land grades, altitude, support and actual circulation remain unverified.
