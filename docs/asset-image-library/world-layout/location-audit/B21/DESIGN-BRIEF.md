## What the image shows

Sea-green stone observatory drum with arched lower facade and wave-edged balcony, giant gold/blue star clock, blue-bronze dome crossed by armillary-like gold rings, stairs and rocky tidal base.

## Proposed layout and experience

A shore path/low bridge reaches a raised entrance deck, wraps along the wave balcony, then climbs to one sheltered viewing chamber or terrace. Keep clock facade facing arrival and retain open tidal views; nearby tide pools provide small discovery stops rather than a new city.
