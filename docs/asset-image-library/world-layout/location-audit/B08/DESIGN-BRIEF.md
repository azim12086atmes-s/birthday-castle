## What the image shows

Oval open court with concentric stepped terraces, repeated cream/gold arched facade bays, several exterior entry stairs, blue drapes and a central blue/gold star medallion. Inner perimeter is planted; source mini-plan confirms oval ring circulation.

## Proposed layout and experience

Choose one clear main arrival, let the inner ring circulate continuously, and connect two secondary stairs to quieter garden overlooks. Keep central wishing court open and readable; place existing wishing objects at discrete edge niches rather than turning every arch into a new room or music venue.

This paragraph describes an imagined, source-compatible arrangement. Hidden backs, room depths, elevations and exact routes are not recovered measurements.
