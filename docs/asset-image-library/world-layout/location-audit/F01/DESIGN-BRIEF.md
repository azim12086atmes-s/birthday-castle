## Proposed layout and experience

Use an entry from Colosseum into mixed oak/beech woods; a fork reaches a stream-pool crossing, then visits FC03 and the quieter FC01/FC02 clearings before looping back through meadow breaks. Alternate enclosed canopy and sunny openings, with one modest bridge and occasional log/rest stops.
