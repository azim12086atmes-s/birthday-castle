# B20 — Glass Bridge Pavilions

[Primary source](../source/B20-GEN-06.png) · [All locations](../README.md) · [Ecology selections](../../location-ecology.csv)

## What the image shows

Circular marble pavilion with very open slender column bays, turquoise/clear glass dome on fine gold ribs, flower garlands and a central pond/fountain. Source palette is delicate glass/gold/marble, not another enclosed domed tower.

## Proposed layout and experience

Set pavilion just off the bridge's main through lane or split the route around its central pool, maintaining one clear continuous passage. Create a small shaded rest ring with views through columns; fountain and plants stay inside the ring. Reuse at distinct bridge stops rather than designing each anew.

This paragraph describes an imagined, source-compatible arrangement. Hidden backs, room depths, elevations and exact routes are not recovered measurements.
