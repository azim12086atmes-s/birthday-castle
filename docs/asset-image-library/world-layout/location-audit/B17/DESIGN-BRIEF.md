## What the image shows

Aged pointed-arch ruin court with broken tower/arch tops, roots and moss climbing stone, pale lilac/blue crystal clusters, star niches and a purple suspended star beneath the main arch. Open voids remain between walls; source is not a sealed chapel.

## Proposed layout and experience

A woodland path crosses a small stone circle into the open court, passes a central wishing/crystal focus, then exits through a broken side arch into a grove. Leave roof absent and preserve framed sky/tree views. Keep vegetation at boundaries so broken edges and routes remain legible.
