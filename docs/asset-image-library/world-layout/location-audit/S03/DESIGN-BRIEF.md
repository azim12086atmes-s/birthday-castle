## What the image shows

WLD-MAP-002 reserves a compact sheltered west-side lake with irregular planted islets. GEN-10 shows calm water, willow/lotus coves, ghats, islands, curved bridge, shore section and lantern night mood, but prints a much larger 3.6 x 1.3 km lake scene.

## Proposed layout and experience

Use a shore loop linking village/forest approaches, one small jetty/ghat, a quiet willow cove and an island viewing spot reached only by a defined bridge/boat route. Preserve calm water and shallow vegetated edge; avoid filling lake with urban quays.
