## What the image shows

## Proposed layout and experience

Let a small meadow path meet the round garden apron and lower doorway, then loop around the tower to a village/forest return. One modest balcony overlooks the clearing; keep planted beds clear of entry and any moving-sail envelope. Use it as an exploration landmark, not a new kitchen or industrial service room.
