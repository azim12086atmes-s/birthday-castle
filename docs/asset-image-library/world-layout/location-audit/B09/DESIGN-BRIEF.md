## What the image shows

Dense ivory/pearl canal settlement with teal domes, stacked roof terraces, arched pedestrian bridges, balconies and water-level entries. Palms and orange-fruited courtyard trees are visibly distinct from woodland planting; mini-plan has multiple water courts/canals.

## Proposed layout and experience

Build one legible arrival quay and a main canal loop connecting three or four courtyard clusters. Footpaths cross on arch bridges while boats remain in lower channels; roof walks return to public stairs rather than dead-end private-looking windows. Vary low water homes, corner houses and taller terrace landmarks.

This paragraph describes an imagined, source-compatible arrangement. Hidden backs, room depths, elevations and exact routes are not recovered measurements.
