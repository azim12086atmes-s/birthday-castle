## What the image shows

Coral-limestone/ivory star shrine with radiating pointed roof/gable forms, small gold finials, shell capitals, central stepped entry and shallow tidal pool surround. Coral-like branches appear as architectural ornament and reef context, not proven living organism assets.

## Proposed layout and experience

One raised reef path meets a dry arrival step, then a small star court beneath the pointed canopy. A low viewing ring watches tidal pools and returns to shore; maintain a clear dry route rather than putting every stair below water.
