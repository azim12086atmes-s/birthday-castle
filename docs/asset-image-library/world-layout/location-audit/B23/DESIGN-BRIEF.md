## What the image shows

Very open seaside pavilion under a turquoise glass/shell canopy with multiple pointed petal lobes, gold ribs, nacre columns/screens and central shell basin. It is not the same circular glass dome as B20.

## Proposed layout and experience

A short shore/dock approach reaches a compact shell courtyard, visitors pass around the basin to a sea-facing balcony and return around the open edge. Keep slender bays and water view unobstructed; a few marine planters soften the landing without filling the hall.
