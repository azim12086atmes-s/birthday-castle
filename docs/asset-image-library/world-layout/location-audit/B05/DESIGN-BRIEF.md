## What the image shows

Amber timber galleries and open pavilions with upturned teal tiled roofs, stacked on unequal rock piers; arched lower bridge, hanging lantern chains, mist and waterfalls. Detailed study differs from the map's pink-spired shorthand.

## Proposed layout and experience

A lower stone bridge brings visitors under rock arch to a stair landing. A winding ascent alternates open timber balconies, sheltered pavilion stops and narrow planted ledges, with a distinct upper lookout. Keep open views between columns; retain uneven heights rather than a flat slab.
