# B14 — Pillar Sky City

[Primary source](../source/B14-GEN-06.png) · [All locations](../README.md) · [Ecology selections](../../location-ecology.csv)

## What the image shows

Unequal sandstone/rock pillars with inhabited garden tops, small open arcade buildings and roofed pavilions, narrow suspended rope bridges and waterfalls down faces. Detailed study is a pillar settlement rather than the map's pink palace-like shorthand.

## Proposed layout and experience

Provide one lower arrival shelf and an accessible ascent to two principal inhabited tops; bridge paths branch to smaller lookout gardens and return through a loop. Keep near/far bridges at distinct levels and preserve open vistas beneath colonnades; use existing travel anchors where physical access is unresolved.

This paragraph describes an imagined, source-compatible arrangement. Hidden backs, room depths, elevations and exact routes are not recovered measurements.

## Assets to assemble

- Rock pillar/platform seat
- Open arcade top building
- Rope bridge anchor/deck
- Ascent/landing interface

Use source-specific paving, cornice, railing, glass, roof and edge profiles; generic nature geometry may share regional materials. Existing motifs should be cropped/isolated before a new decor design.

**Flora:** Hanging vines, planted canopy pockets, sheltered shrubs.

**Fauna:** Small roosting birds and butterflies.

**Small objects:** Choose only useful lamps, benches, planters, route signs and wish objects; existing GEN-20/28/35 families are reuse candidates, not universal style matches.

Shared water, rock, soil, bank and scatter families are indexed in the [general natural catalog](../../shared-natural-kit-catalog.csv). They receive local material/placement choices; they do not flatten every habitat into the same look.

## Reuse and unresolved work

Need rock-to-platform footings, open arcade/corner hosts, platform edge/rail segments, rope-bridge anchor/deck details and stair-ascent joins. Do not repurpose porcelain bridge deck as rope bridge. Hanging vines and sparse canopy pockets; roosting birds/butterflies proposed. Elevation/access/collision unresolved.
