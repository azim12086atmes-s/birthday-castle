## What the image shows

Twin pearl-stone gateway towers with gold domes, pointed blue/gold window screens, a high open central arch, lanterns/garlands and broad front steps. Tiny iso suggests a repeated gateway group; the central sea view is a key feature.

## Proposed layout and experience

Connect a sheltered cove landing to the gate apron, then pass under the arch toward the principal bridge. Side tower balconies become small lookout pockets rather than closing the harbor vista. Place compact planted strips, lamps and route signs outside the through lane.
