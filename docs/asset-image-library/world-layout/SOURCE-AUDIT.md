## Scale and placements

The initial expansion was2.5→7.5km in each direction. After the owner confirmed a2.5km palace, B01 width was corrected to2500m, depth2125m provisionally. B25 moves to(-1400,-1400) andB18 to(-1550,1500), outside the proposed250m palace landscape band. [Width correction](PALACE-SCALE-CORRECTION.md) preserves that arithmetic and its provisional assumptions.

The owner then requested forests and forest cottages. The new12km envelope expands land around three outer woodlands while retaining the corrected core placements. Core dimensions and heights are not scaled. R02/R03 external starting junctions move from inside the palace reservation to(-1550,-600)/(1550,-600); actual palace access remains unresolved. LayoutJSON preserves earlier B11/B17/B18/B25 relocation history.

F01–F03,FC01–FC08,FT01–FT03 andFD01–FD03 are new proposed district/placement/route identifiers. They are not registered mesh IDs. Cottage form variants reuseB12, with no new dedicated image allowance per instance. Proposed shorelines and forest polygons are planning envelopes, not recovered source measurements or generated terrain geometry.

Sky sites remain horizontal projections; S03 is a ground inland lake andS06 a ground coastal meadow. Sky arrival altitudes, waterlevels and river grades remain unresolved.
