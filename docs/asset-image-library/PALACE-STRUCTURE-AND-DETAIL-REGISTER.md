# Palace structural families and matching detail kits

Use the original palace front GEN-37 as the overall layout authority, with GEN-03 and GEN-16 as supporting views. Use the selected rose/ivory marble, indigo/blue/lilac/pink glass, gold-edged lotus/leaf carving and plum roof vocabulary consistently. Source boards listed below are existing references, not claims that every part is isolated or ready for 3D generation.

The palace has continuous stepped wards, roof gardens, recessed and projecting wall returns, rounded/chamfered wing ends and asymmetrical tower groups. Do not replace that structure with disconnected boxes or a generic repeated facade. Preserve the established gate, central cascade and dry flanking stair routes.

## Current close-up: portal pier and arch springing

The GEN-29 crop is a perspective assembly detail: three clustered shafts, flaring lotus capitals, adjoining arch ribs and stained-glass fields behind. It does not show a cut through the material, so it is not a cross-section. It does not establish the whole wall, full arch extent, base placement or exact depth. A full view is a reconstruction of that missing extent, using the observed component vocabulary and the source's existing base/capital views.

For the full view, treat the glazed bays as real window apertures with recessed glazing, supported by a wall/frame of visible thickness. Do not imply an open walk-through portal where glass closes the bay. Record inferred backs, widths and joints as design proposals. Structural assemblies and their detail meshes are separate catalogue layers, and both must be checked during modelling.

Generation remains missing-only. This register is an audit map, not authorization to regenerate all the referenced boards or a claim that those entire families are absent.
