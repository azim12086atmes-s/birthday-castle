# Initial modular palace wall references

These three sheets were generated before the owner paused the batch and switched to direct screenshots. They remain **unapproved references**, not completed meshes or a palace construction plan.

- PACK-PAL-WALL-L1: four lower-arcade wall sections.
- PACK-PAL-WALL-L2: four great-window/gate wall sections.
- PACK-PAL-WALL-UP: four gallery wall sections proposed for reuse at L3/L4.

The source glass and carved-detail families are reused through reuse-bindings.json. Proposed body dimensions and end profiles are in interfaces.json; actual snap geometry requires model inspection and normalization. The images do not certify joint fit.

The active workflow now develops **one screenshot family at a time**, beginning with the terminal tower. A complete section-by-section palace inventory is deferred. No Blender models or paid mesh calls were produced for these sheets.
