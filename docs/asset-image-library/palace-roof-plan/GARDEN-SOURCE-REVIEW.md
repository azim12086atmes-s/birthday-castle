## Corrections to the previous roof plan

The previous coordinate drawing placed generic rectangular courts with interchangeable small pools. It omitted the eastern conservatory, the connected lotus/star water feature, the north loggia and the irregular moon pond. Its horizontal path stripes did not establish continuous routes between stair landings. Decorative crowns also made the plan harder to read.

The replacement is a plain overhead diagram: circles mark all 33 inventoried tower placements, outlines mark garden zones and structural returns, simple shapes mark water and buildings, and tread rectangles mark 11 dry stair links. Thin dashed lines connect access points on the same roof level. The five south bays remain on one continuous gate wall; G3 is the entrance.

Garden-zone coordinates are **new fitting proposals within the existing working roof envelope**, not measured transfers from the illustrated boards. Tower coordinates, wall contours, confirmed setbacks and roof heights are retained. The overall envelope, all tower depths and the extra upper-front setback remain proposed. No claim is made that these illustrations form mutually registered elevations.

GEN-16 labels the lotus fountain as 18 m wide and the mirror pool as 30 × 12 m; GEN-14/16 show 3 m clear walks and GEN-15 shows 3–6 m walking routes. These are local feature references. They must not be blindly multiplied by 2.75 or expanded to fill giant terrace bands. The site drawing uses enlarged fixture symbols for readability; final furniture and walking widths require a separate construction layout.

This is a revision of the existing PAL-ROOF-PLAN-001 allowance, not another selected sheet. The earlier drawing and its data remain in superseded/R02. The final library cap remains 230 and the palace cap remains 65.
