# FC05 Function observations

- The coarse doorway height and reused ceiling contract conflict. The prototype records the original 2.6 m proposal and its separate fanlight/2.1 m passage alternative; neither image interpretation nor a passing walk silently approves the change.
- The shared room recipe needed configurable house/placement/route data, not scaled furniture or copied cottage source files. Existing village defaults remain covered by their geometry and browser regression checks.
- Sampling water height at the exact edge of a strict wet-region test produced jagged rill faces. The water mesh now interpolates the saved centreline levels independently of the wet/dry containment predicate.
- A fixed 500 ms keyboard hold was unreliable under software rendering. Browser verification now focuses the scene and waits for observed player movement while the key is held. Full route checks still use collision-driven motion, not endpoint teleports.
- The first preview exceeded the proposed triangle allowance. Reducing the ground grid to 128 subdivisions brought the arrival observation to 89,822 triangles / 45 draws; independent route probes retain the documented 12 cm mesh/surface tolerance.

These are local observations, not approved general lessons. No Function, Form or Runtime approval is inferred. Exact cottage architecture, roof/weather structure, sleeping furniture, final natural-object fidelity and production-device performance remain unresolved.
