# Bedroom Function observations

- The room board includes contradictory printed dimensions and a rail across one perspective approach. The saved correction schedule, accepted sample coordinates and separate bed/balcony sheets control the fixture.
- The original coarse vault sketch did not specify a side-wall springline that accommodated the 12 m west opening. This fixture proposes 12.6 m while retaining the 18 m apex; final construction remains unapproved.
- Early gathered curtains used a tubular silhouette that read as rope. They were replaced with pleated fabric surfaces and visible attachment lines, preserving the flat source dimensions separately from their proposed posed envelopes.
- Floating-point endpoint error could send a pointed surround vertex to infinity. Aperture containment now has an endpoint tolerance; every mesh position and normal is independently checked for finiteness.
- Balcony flower pots need actual support, rather than decorative placement beside the rail. Bracket plates and rail attachments are indexed and tested with the planting geometry. Their additional geometry pushed a corner view beyond the 160,000-triangle prototype allowance; reducing redundant tube sampling preserved silhouettes and restored budget headroom.

- Route collision success alone did not prove a drawn support surface beneath the south apron. The apron substrate is now explicit, and every return-route segment is checked with downward mesh rays. The reading chaise also rests on the actual rug top instead of penetrating it.

These are local observations, not approved general lessons. Final decorative fidelity, cloth/hinges, glass, balcony/roof engineering, palace placement and production performance remain pending.
