## Construction limits

The sheets add **32 logical component targets**, all unregistered as 3D models. Pairs and clusters may contain several meshes. The packed illustrations do not prove that a generator will separate every part; check labels, members and geometry on intake.

Interfaces propose 3 m wall/roof repeats, a 6 m roof span, door/window apertures, 6 m trail patches and 3 m path ports. **S04’s small roof end sketches resemble filled panels.** Build an open-ended roof substrate from the interface contract rather than copying that infill. Corner sealing, footing overlap, floors, chimney flashing, shutter fit, UVs and physical joins remain unverified. Dimensions are proposals, not recovered measurements.

The placement guide gives local arrival offsets for FC04–FC06. FT02’s world-map centre points are coarse clearing markers; actual paths must pass outside cottage footprints and connect through short entrance spurs. A 70 m clearing radius describes a natural landscape gap, not a giant paved plaza.
