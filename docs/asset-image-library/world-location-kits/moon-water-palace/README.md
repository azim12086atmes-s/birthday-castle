## Source and local character

GEN-05’s native B02 panel is the architectural authority: pearl onion dome, ivory/cream marble with restrained blush veins, gold edging and lattice, amber/lilac portal glass, pale blue accents, four slender corner minarets and smaller side pavilions. The tiny GEN-04 map facade has a blue roof; it informs location only. These sheets expand small visible motifs into usable component views; hidden faces, precise ornament repeats and dimensions are inferred. They do not recover exact construction from the low-resolution source.

The repeated pier/window/cornice language stays lighter than the original main palace. Marble texture, ornament sharpness and inset dimensions remain reference proposals. Avoid copying the original palace’s purple domes or oversaturated large Gothic rose windows into B02.

## Proposed site layout and kit use

Retain the source’s central south-facing reflecting-water axis. A broad ghat rises to the entrance platform; circulation splits along two shaded arcade walks around water, reaching small planted courts and smaller dome pavilions beside the main hall. Keep dry walking routes outside lotus beds, benches/urns clear of the path and willow roots outside foundations. Perimeter and rear returns may use the L-corner and arcade repeats; their exact counts and routes need a locked site plan.

Use a procedural hall shell and terrace/platform substrate. Mount the portal wall into it, cut the lower door opening, place the central drum/dome above it and corner minarets on supported footings. Shafts repeat vertically beneath a crown; count/spacing derives from the source silhouette after a common assembly scale is agreed. Smaller pavilions may reuse scaled roof forms with separately checked human circulation. The eight sections are building blocks, not a complete reconstructed building or all interior assets.

The world’s210 × 150 m reservation and210 m legacy height remain unchanged, provisional. Working component dimensions are not a validated fit to those numbers. Lock one architectural assembly scale first, then fit inserts to hosts. Keep benches, lanterns, plants and visitors at their own human/natural sizes rather than multiplying all props with the architecture.

## Assembly constraints

Matching18 m dome/drum rims and2.8 m shaft/crown rims are numerical contracts only. Adjacent arcade bays must share one end pier; do not leave doubled columns. Reconcile drum windows with D09 and portal glass with its host after cutters are locked. D03 is a smaller arch surround proposal, not a proven fit to S01’s taller opening. Preserve an open lower entrance under upper glazing.

The ghat’s1.2 m label is floor rise, excluding cheek/parapet height. Eight0.15 m risers,0.45 m treads and2.4 m upper landing are proposed construction values; drawn tread count is not proof. Plain stair substrate should be built from these values, with ornament added later. Cornices/parapets need square end faces and shared profiles. D12’s2 m arc at2.1 m radius does not close a full ring by integer repetition: trim or resample the ring pattern.

## Reuse and remaining gaps

GEN-13 supplies lotus, lilies, reeds, low flowering plants and bank vegetation; GEN-09 supplies willow. GEN-20 provides existing benches, urns and lighting. Water/pond/fountain/path references remain shared families to inspect when their assembly is needed. Existing dragonfly reference can be reused; fish/ducks are still missing and belong to the shared freshwater fauna allowance, not another B02 building sheet. Natural water, rock and bank geometry are not repeated as a new kit for every location.

Roof/host internals, unseen backs, repeatable paving textures, lower minaret footings, water levels, interior rooms, fine screen repeats, actual mesh joins, UV seams, collision and structural support remain unbuilt. Three building sheets use the full B02 allowance; resolve minor missing profiles procedurally or by an explicitly reviewed budget reallocation rather than automatically adding a fourth.

## Reference review

**B02-STRUCT-001**

Four labelled groups and their main/front/plan/side views are present. Arcade and corner arches visibly open; portal lower passage remains clear. Pearl/ivory hosts, gold mouldings and pale blue/amber accents derive from GEN-05, with extra resolved ornament proposed.

All metrics and hidden depth are working proposals. The depicted stairs do not prove eight identical risers;1.2 m is the stair floor rise, excluding raised cheek/parapet ornament. Cornices, pier caps and inset elevations are not identical measured projections.

A repeated arcade must share one boundary pier, avoiding doubled columns or overlapping floors. Model the shell and cut openings first; retain trims as removable groups. Arcade roof/depth closure, exact L-corner footprint, stairs and hall rear joins require model review.

**B02-ROOF-001**

Four labelled roof/tower sections are present: pearl onion shell, drum ring, repeat shaft and open crown. Main dome has a hollow underside and tip socket; crown shows open column-supported lantern. Revision removes opaque drum window infill and apparent cap.

Eighteen-metre dome/drum interfaces and2.8 m shaft/crown interfaces match numerically. They are proposed nominal dimensions, not evaluated mesh diameters. Crown brackets, dome wall thickness, lantern interior and circular seam profiles are not verified.

Small front/side/underside/section insets are explanatory drawings, not true projections of one model. Drum window sizes differ visually from the standalone D09 proposal; reconcile aperture and insert together. Main roof fringe and drum crown need one shared seat detail so stacked decorative rims do not collide.
