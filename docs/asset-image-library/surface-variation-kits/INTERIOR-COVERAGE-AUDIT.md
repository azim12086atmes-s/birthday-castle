## Required sheet structure

For each room, first establish one coordinated multi-view sheet: entrance view, reverse view, side elevation, longitudinal section, straight-up ceiling/reflected-ceiling view and two opposed ceiling obliques. Keep the room outline, openings, columns, galleries and stairs consistent. Unseen geometry is a proposed extension of the source, not an exact recovered design.

Then make a structural sheet with 3–4 complete reusable ceiling/wall assemblies where those are missing. Put missing small removable ornaments into 10–20-item labelled sheets. Reuse existing floors, glass, capitals, cornices and lights by ID rather than drawing them again. Interior wall faces and roof undersides need mounting profiles and junctions, not just ornament close-ups.

**Pointed vaults, flat coffers, glazed conservatory roofs and dome interiors are separate construction types.** A purple onion crown outside does not prove that the interior has the same outline. Tower-dome interiors remain undetermined and must be marked as proposals.

The sequence is ballroom → throne room → library → conservatory, one room/reference at a time; then source-compatible tower-dome interiors and needed private-room references. Rose/ivory marble, fine gold, blue/lilac/pink glass and existing motifs remain the palette. Human-scale furnishings need their own scale, not the palace exterior scale multiplier.

## Scope and budget

Baths/spas, kitchens/bakeries, service rooms and music spaces are excluded by the owner. Their previous candidate descriptions remain in the JSON as excluded history. The unproduced INT-R04 bath reservation is retired without removing saved art. Palace cap65 and global target230 remain unchanged. This audit does not reserve or generate new sheets.
