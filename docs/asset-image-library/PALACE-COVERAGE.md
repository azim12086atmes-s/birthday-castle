## Window selection matters

GEN-19's three-light lancet is an existing variant. It must not silently replace the selected front's great-bay pattern. GEN-38 M02 displays two repeated examples; select **one** as a single great-window bay rather than doubling every bay. Use M04 for small gallery windows, M03 for terminal star panels and the source-correct M01 for the gate. The new body sheets provide apertures/seats so the existing designs can be added in 3D, rather than commissioning new window patterns.

## Scope and placement rules

- Keep individual level rises 60/100/70/70 m in the historic grid, cumulative roofs 60/160/230/300 m; the inherited 2.75 scale gives 165/440/632.5/825 m. Generic prop labels do not override these building levels.
- Keep the approved gate/stair setbacks and source front composition. Rear footprints, wall depths, module junction dimensions and exact snap fits are unresolved design/model tasks.
- Structural shells are continuous stepped ranges with rounded/chamfered projections. They are not disconnected boxes lined up side by side.
- Reuse the existing gardens, fountains, rails, glass and carving families on those shells. Local repeated modules do not force a symmetrical whole palace.
- Props and private rooms use human-scale dimensions within the monumental envelope. No Blender model, mesh separation, topology or runtime performance is certified by these reference sheets.

The nine planned sheets are capped reservations, not automatic approvals. Generate each only against its documented gap. If an adequate original is found, cancel its reservation rather than duplicate it.
