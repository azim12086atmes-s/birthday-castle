## Rear extension

Use a proposed **30 grid m rear strip (82.5 world m at ×2.75)**. This is a working allowance chosen under the owner's request for a slightly longer castle. Extend away from the ceremonial front, keeping its width, bay rhythm, projecting ends and vertical proportions. Align neighboring rear seams; do not add the strip three times when west, centre and east parts join. Continue wall thickness, roof substrate and side returns through the added length. Preserve exposed terrace strips, circulation and small garden/fountain fittings.

The illustration supplies no measured starting rear depth. This allowance therefore does not establish a final footprint or exact snap interfaces. The old model's huge rear footprint is not adopted by these sheets. Do not make the rear extension climb into another hill of buildings or move the main tower groups to the back edge.

## Fixed relationships and reused designs

- L1 front to gate: 150 grid m. Stair top to gate: 75 grid m. L2 to L3 setback: 60 grid m.
- L4 front setback remains unresolved; an old 120 grid m position was a proposal.
- Two great window bays west and two east of one central gate. A paired glass detail must not become two arches inside every single bay.
- The broad western onion pavilion, eastern belvedere and right-hand spire cluster retain their original lateral relationships. Hidden depths require modelling review.
- The central wet cascade and both dry flanking stairs retain their source arrangement.
- Reuse GEN-19/38 glazing, GEN-29/41 lotus piers and capitals, GEN-39 rails, GEN-42 cornices, GEN-43 lower wall/stair/water ornament and GEN-45 metal fittings. GEN-16 roof-garden fittings and GEN-17/32/44 crowns remain existing designs.

## Review before modelling

Compare each generated front to its exact source regions, then check that its front, overhead and three-quarter views agree. Record wrong bay counts, changed curved returns, misplaced tower attachments, disconnected wings, altered cascade geometry and inconsistent depth arrows. A beautiful sheet is not evidence that these checks passed. No Blender assembly or paid image-to-3D submission is included in this pass.
